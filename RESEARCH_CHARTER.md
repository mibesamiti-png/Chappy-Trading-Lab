# Research charter and detailed roadmap (revision 1)
2026-11-27 JST | Coordinator guidance supplementing ROADMAP.md. Read with AUTONOMY_RULES.md. This does not change current task ownership or unlock any holdout.

## 1. Purpose and success
The owner's long-term motivation is to determine whether a genuinely useful, economically viable research direction exists. This repository's present work is **academic/educational market research only**. HF Data Library inputs may not be used to develop a real-trading algorithm or generate trading signals. A future commercial track requires separate authorization, independently licensed data, a fresh protocol and independent validation; do not carry restricted data, derived features/models, findings or code tailored to the restricted dataset into such a track unless the relevant rights explicitly permit it. No orders or brokerage integration.
The academic objective is to quantify what is and is not predictable in observed market time series, explain possible mechanisms and artifacts, and test reproducibility. A well-supported null result is a successful scientific outcome. Profitability is not an acceptance criterion for this academic track.

## 2. Research questions and ordering
| ID | Question | Evidence required | Interpretation limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Are reported phenomena real statistical effects or measurement artifacts? | Audit time alignment, Clean transformations, missingness, observation filters, duplicate handling, session boundaries, baselines and uncertainty | IEX is partial coverage; minute OHLCV alone cannot establish quote-level mechanisms |
| Q2 | What is predictable: direction, absolute variation, or observed activity? | Separate target definitions, naive baselines, out-of-time uncertainty and effect sizes | Variation or volume prediction does not establish direction prediction or profitability |
| Q3 | How do effects vary with time aggregation and observable context? | Pre-registered 1/5/15/30-minute comparisons, observation coverage and calendar sensitivity, fixed conditions | No filling missing bars with invented trades; no overlapping-window leakage |
| Q4 | Do conditional effects add information beyond simple baselines? | Restricted hypothesis family, nested development comparisons, multiplicity corrections and negative controls | No endless feature search or treating correlation as causation |
| Q5 | Is simple learning scientifically justified? | Independent development validation, documented robustness and improvement over a relevant baseline | Models optional; no fitting for live use, no claims of causal or real-time predictability from noncausal Clean inputs |
| Q6 | Does the frozen academic result reproduce in the reserved period? | Owner-approved protocol, one sealed evaluation, complete reporting including failures | No post-hoc retuning on 2026; a failed holdout remains a failed holdout |
| Q7 | What can be independently reproduced and what remains unknown? | Complete audit trail, deterministic code/config, synthetic tests, limitations and closure | Publish only explicitly permitted material |

## 3. Detailed stage gates
- **R2 / CTL-002 (audit):** recover exact previous methodology and baseline metrics from local artifacts. Confirm split boundaries, whether 2024/2025 were repeatedly consulted, test family and dependence, sample selection, missing minutes, adjusted prices, and Clean lookahead. Separate findings from hypotheses; label unavailable bid/ask or broader market evidence as unidentifiable. Do not pretend a causal data source can be recovered by merely shifting noncausally cleaned bars. If leakage cannot be excluded, downgrade to descriptive research.
- **R3 / CTL-003 (pre-registration):** freeze the hypothesis family, sampling/exclusion criteria, session and aggregation definitions, target/baseline for each question, effect-size and uncertainty rules, multiple testing method, robustness checks, finite search budget and stop rule BEFORE executing the comparisons. All choices should be justified from prior knowledge or prior development results. 2024/2025 have already been consulted, so neither is a fresh final holdout. Reserve 2026; use only <=2025 for exploration. Record protocol hash and version. Thresholds not set by the owner are research defaults to justify before testing, never adjust after seeing validation results.
- **R4 / CTL-004 (bounded tests):** implement local reproducible statistics on the frozen protocol. Check simulated null/known-effect and leakage fixtures, compare aggregate horizons without crossing sessions, quantify effective observations and coverage, use dependence-aware uncertainty (e.g. day/block resampling where appropriate), compare to fixed baseline, preserve all negative results. Report sensitivity to exclusion of known incomplete dates, short sessions, sparse data and other predeclared quality strata. Keep detailed results local. If the audit shows real-time inference is impossible, run only explicitly retrospective/descriptive comparisons.
- **R5 / CTL-005 (decision):** review the predeclared gate and compare evidence with added complexity. If nothing meets it, record null result and close that branch. If evidence justifies a simple academic learning study, first freeze a bounded protocol and obtain approval for any scope expansion; compare simple interpretable research models, feature availability, calibration and robustness on development data only. No automatic progression to a general learning-engine project. A separate Training Lab is an optional future project, not a current task.
- **R6 / CTL-006 (final gate):** obtain explicit owner approval and freeze protocol, code, config, model (if applicable), metrics and decision rules. Evaluate reserved 2026 once, without changing the protocol. Record all outputs locally and public-safe pass/fail status. If no justified frozen hypothesis exists, do not open holdout merely to complete the roadmap.
- **R7 (synthesis):** report supported and unsupported claims, effect uncertainty, bias, coverage, reproducibility, open questions and stop decision. Do not present academic evidence as a trading strategy or guarantee. Record any proposed future work separately, for owner review.

## 4. Evidence and resource discipline
Each task has a finite hypothesis/test budget and a completion/stop condition. Reuse verified indices, tests and intermediate summaries; use streaming/partitioned computation locally instead of feeding raw rows into an AI context. Record only essential status publicly; never commit provider data, private results, paths, manifests or trained artifacts. No raw/restricted data in GitHub issues, PRs, logs or CI. Use synthetic fixtures for public testing.
Do not optimize a model, metric or reporting format merely because an earlier attempt failed. A new test must identify a distinct question and expected evidence. Stop if effect is not robust, data cannot identify it, budget exhausted, or approval needed. Do not spend the remaining budget just to stay active.
Work may advance through authorized ready tasks in a run, but cannot bypass dependencies, the locked holdout, or license boundaries. Prefer current active PR for state updates. The coordinator can review sanitized methods and status; detailed artifacts stay in the authorized local environment.
