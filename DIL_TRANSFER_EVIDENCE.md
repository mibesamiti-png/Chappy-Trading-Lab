**Evidence classes:** E = measured in the original stated experiment (not proof in CTL); P = proposal / unvalidated behavioral or operational benefit; A = new CTL adaptation (not CTL measurement). Negative results are retained. DIL studies largely use researcher-authored artificial cases, not independent blind LLM experiments.
